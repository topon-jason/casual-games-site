FIVE-GAME PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

UPDATED OCTOBER 22, 2026

This policy applies to Neon Lane Switch, Meteor Tap Escape, Color Gate Rush, Pocket Orbit Dash, and Crystal Stack Drop. Each game works without an account, and its core gameplay is available offline.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked levels, scores, and collected items are stored locally on your device. The games do not operate a cloud account system and do not upload this local game progress to the developer.

Advertising

The games use TopOn to mediate rewarded video and interstitial advertising. Depending on the ad served, TopOn and an advertising partner may process technical and advertising information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app and ad placement identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention data.

The mediation configuration may include AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Chartboost, Digital Turbine, InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff Monetize, Mintegral, Pangle, Unity Ads, and Kwai. The active partner may vary by region, availability, and auction result. These companies process information under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

On supported Apple devices, iOS may ask whether you allow tracking. If you decline, the game does not receive access to the device advertising identifier and requests non-personalized advertising where supported. Declining tracking does not remove gameplay features. You may change this choice later in iOS Settings.

Rewarded video is optional. Interstitial ads may appear after a run ends or at another natural break. No banner or native advertising format is used.

How information is used

Advertising partners may use the information described above to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, maintain service reliability, and—where permitted—personalize advertising.

Sharing and international processing

Advertising information may be shared among TopOn, the partner serving an ad, measurement providers, service providers, and legal authorities when required. These providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply their own retention periods and safeguards.

Children

These games are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising partner, use the contact path below so the request can be routed appropriately.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or to object to certain advertising uses. Requests concerning data controlled by an advertising partner may need to be made directly to that partner.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the games, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the relevant App Store product page and include the game name and “Privacy” in the subject. See the Support page for request guidance.
