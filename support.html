PLAYER SUPPORT

Get back into the game.

This support page covers Neon Lane Switch, Meteor Tap Escape, Color Gate Rush, Pocket Orbit Dash, and Crystal Stack Drop.

An ad is not ready

Check your network connection, wait a moment, and try again. Ad availability varies by country and provider. A missing ad does not block ordinary offline gameplay.

A reward was not added

A reward is granted only after the advertising provider reports that the video was completed. Closing early or losing the connection may prevent the reward.

The game paused

The game pauses when iOS sends it to the background. Return to the game and choose Resume to continue when that option is available.

Progress appears reset

Progress is stored locally. Removing the app, clearing its data, or restoring without app data may remove saved levels and scores.

Change tracking permission

Open iOS Settings, choose Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Core gameplay does not require tracking permission.

Report an inappropriate ad

Use the information or privacy control displayed inside the ad when available. Include a screenshot, approximate time, country, device model, and game name in a support request.

Contact

Use the Contact Developer address on the relevant App Store product page. Include the game name, app version, iOS version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, full advertising identifiers, or payment details.
